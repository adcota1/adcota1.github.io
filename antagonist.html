Antagonists

Your antagonist should believe they're right

The antagonist in your story must beleive in the cause they're fighting for. Whether their actions are inherently evil or not, they must beleive they're justified in their actions.

Make them capable

An antagonist should be a direct threat to the protagonist. They should be as capable or more, but never weaker than the protagonist. This must be the case regardless of the ability the story needs.

Connect to the main themes

The antagonist must make the protagonist struggle in an aspect of themselves they must overcome. A good antagonist invites fear, doubt, failure, and exposure of the protagonists weaknesses and insecurities.
