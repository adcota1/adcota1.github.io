Protagonists

Protagonists need to be active

A protagonist that doesn't make their own decisions throughout the story makes the reader feel like the story is happening to them instead of coming from what they choose to do. Always make an attempt to make your protagonist actively be a part of the events that are happening.

A flawed protagonist is an interesting one

Protagonists must have flaws in order for the reader to relate to them. It's in a character's vulnerabilities that we as readers are able to connect with them. Be sure to give flaws that make sense with the story you're telling.

Goals push the story

Your protagonist must have clear goals that drive the story forward. A protagonist who lacks a clear goal will not be able to make decisions according to that goal. They must want it bad enough to begin change.
