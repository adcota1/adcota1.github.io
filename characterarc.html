Character Arcs

Designing a Character Arc

When desgigning a character arc, you must consider the character's purpose and how they connect to both the worldbuilding and themes.

Character Arc Types

There are three main types of character arcs, choose the one that best fits your character!

Character Arc Type Description Recommendation
Positive Arc A character that learns and grows as a result of their experiences Great for protagonists or supporting characters that need to learn something throughout the story
Negative Arc A character beleives a lie that causes them to regress or become worse Suitable for antagonists or characters that serve as warnings
Flat Arc A character who remains unchanged in their beliefs but change the conflict and characters around them Ideal for protagonists of long running series or characters that don't need any development
